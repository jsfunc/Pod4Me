# Device acceptance checks

These checks are pending. Run them on the Galaxy S24 / One UI 9 after a successful build. Android controls and background behavior cannot be verified solely by source inspection or JVM tests.

1. Install `dist/Pod4Me.apk`, open it, and verify content respects display cutouts, status/navigation bars, dark mode, larger text, and rotation.
2. Subscribe to two feeds. Import duplicates alongside an invalid URL; valid subscriptions should survive the reported error. Rename a feed, refresh, and confirm the custom name survives. Export and inspect the `url [podcast title]` lines, then import a mixture of labeled lines and plain URLs; only the URLs should be read.
3. Download on Wi-Fi and mobile data, background the app, and confirm completion is visible on returning. Cancel and retry a download. Test lost connectivity, an HTTP error, and insufficient storage.
4. Add a custom list, rename it, assign it as a feed default, and download another episode. Check Unheard, feed view, and the default list. Delete the custom list and confirm the audio remains.
5. Rate multiple episodes and check all five sorts, including 0% first and rated-only Starred. Change the sort in Starred and verify it stays in Starred. Check a rated expired episode can be found in Starred and downloaded again.
6. Play an episode and put the app in the background, lock the phone, and use another app. Verify audio focus during a call and pause when headphones disconnect. Check the Android media panel's progress, play/pause, previous/next, and ±10-second buttons. Available button placement is controlled by One UI.
7. Change speed, pause, close/reopen, and resume. Verify global speed and episode position. Add named and unnamed bookmarks, rename and delete them, and seek to bookmarks from Player and Details.
8. Seek to 94%, then 95%, and verify the Unheard-to-Heard transition occurs only at the threshold. Verify removing from Unheard marks heard. Repeated progress updates must not extend the seven-day deadline.
9. Add episodes to a queue in a different order from the library, reorder/remove queue entries while playing, and verify manual Next, automatic completion, and source-list fallback. Explicitly queue a previously played episode. Stop at the end of the list, then verify Play restarts the final episode.
10. Protect a download with 4ever; verify Delete is disabled and launch cleanup preserves it. Remove protection and verify deletion keeps stars/bookmarks/history. Stop the current episode before deleting it.
11. For expiry tests, use a disposable emulator or test database with a past expiry. Launch with ordinary, protected, and currently playing expired downloads; only the ordinary inactive download should be removed. Do not change your everyday phone's clock for testing.
12. Send an episode suggestion via WhatsApp/email, then share that text into Pod4Me. Verify its title, audio URL, and optional feed subscription. Repeat for a feed and a plain audio URL. Confirm cancellation causes no download/subscription and export contains no shared-audio pseudo-feed.
13. Force-stop while downloading and relaunch after completion. Verify the completed file is reconciled once. Reopen after process death and confirm queue, lists, ratings, bookmarks, and playback positions are retained.
14. Upgrade the existing installation to 1.0.0 using the same signing key. Verify subscriptions, downloaded files, ratings, bookmarks, and lists survive. Refresh feeds and check artwork in Feeds, Library, Queue, Latest, and next to the player's title. Test feeds without images, an unavailable image URL, and previously cached images while offline.
15. Check Previous, ±10 seconds, Play/Pause, and Next stay in a single row on the S24. Verify compact feed episode rows show a download arrow and a question-mark details action, and that screen readers announce each icon's purpose.
16. In Feeds, verify Browse/Settings/Refresh sit beside the artwork/title; larger artwork spans both rows on the left; episode counts, expiry and smaller Share/bin icons sit on the second row to its right. Tap the bin and cancel, then tap it again and confirm. The subscription should disappear from Feeds/Latest/export and be skipped by Refresh; its downloads, queued tracks, ratings, bookmarks and 4ever protection must remain. Re-import its URL and confirm that it reuses the saved feed and custom settings. Repeat while a refresh is in flight.
17. In Queue, use the arrows and bin beside the title. Verify the first Up and last Down controls are disabled, reordering changes playback order, and removing an entry does not delete its audio. Long-press icons to check their labels.
18. Import an RSS feed larger than 8 MB and verify its final episode is present. Run `./gradlew :app:connectedDebugAndroidTest` with the phone/emulator connected for Android-specific streaming-parser and import/export checks. In Latest, verify that artwork spans the full item height, with title, date, played percentage and expiry to its right; Library remains unchanged.
19. In Library, check that Delete, Play, Add to queue and Details icons fit on one row with tighter spacing and sit to the right of the date/progress/expiry, with `41%` and a short expiry such as `Expires 28 Nov`. Check 4ever and no-expiry episodes, long titles, large text settings, and expired Starred episodes offering Download in place of Play. Full dates should remain visible in Details.

20. At 96%, mark the current episode Unheard. It must seek to zero and remain Unheard after pausing, waiting ten seconds, backgrounding and reopening. Repeat with a feed that declares an incorrect duration; the measured audio length should determine progress.
21. Start from the middle of a downloaded list. Previous should immediately reach the preceding episode. Check artwork and ±10-second buttons in the system player. Close the service/process normally (not Force stop), then press Bluetooth/headset Play; verify saved position, global speed, queue and fallback order. Force stop disables system resumption until the app is opened again.
22. Stop playback, back up the library from Feeds, alter a rating, and restore. Verify metadata, lists, bookmarks, queue and settings; the backup rating should be restored, existing audio kept, and a repeated restore should not duplicate entries. On a clean emulator, restore the same file and confirm no audio downloads automatically, missing queue entries can be downloaded, and subsequent shared audio imports work.
23. Try a truncated or edited invalid backup. Confirm the library is unchanged. Restore is refused while current playback is loaded; Stop enables it.
24. Download successfully, delete that audio, and verify Android's corresponding download record is removed. Repeat completion reconciliation and confirm no duplicate memberships or refreshed expiry dates.
25. With a large library, scroll Latest/Feeds while playback runs, open Details, switch to Starred and change sorting. Check that the list remains responsive and storage usage updates after download/deletion. Use Android Studio Profiler for on-device memory/CPU checks; JVM timing is not a phone benchmark.
26. Verify the header remains one line with `Pod4Me`, `used: N - free: N GB`, and Refresh. Confirm v1 installed as an update without losing audio or metadata; the build script must report a non-debuggable APK and matching certificate.

27. In Feeds, Latest and Queue, verify artwork fills each item’s content height and text/controls remain usable with long titles, errors and large font settings. Confirm bottom navigation has five icon-only buttons of equal height and icon size, and TalkBack announces each destination.

28. In Downloads, tap the bin and cancel, then confirm deletion. Confirm only audio and the queue entry are removed; ratings, bookmarks, lists and history remain (use Starred or Latest to inspect the episode). Check the bin is disabled for 4ever and current playing/paused episodes. Check all four controls fit with large text and long metadata. Verify the Feeds microphone appears comparable in size to the other bottom icons. These new controls have not yet been tested on a phone.

29. In Feeds, Browse a feed and tap Search. Find an undownloaded episode by title, then by a word found only in its description. Try mixed case, accents omitted, reordered words, prefixes and a one-letter typo/transposition. Confirm stronger title matches appear above weaker description/typo matches, with newer episodes first on ties. Download, play and open Details from results. Clear or close search and verify newest-first order returns. Switch feeds quickly while typing and confirm no stale results appear from another feed/query. Search offline and while playback runs; check keyboard, large text, rotation, empty/no-match states and responsiveness on a large feed. Refresh a changed feed and verify the open search updates. Install 1.0.1 over this computer’s 1.0.0 build and verify library/audio preservation. **All of these device checks remain pending.**
